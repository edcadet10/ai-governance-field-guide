# Give the IT lead a list they can act on

The reviewer collects evidence from the information owner and the IT lead. IT provides an approved access export or demonstrates the current setting. The **IT lead makes every system change**. Use a restricted register for real folder paths, group membership, guest identities and evidence links. Leadership sees counts, risk, owner and decision date.

1. **Identify the location and owner.** Use a short reference for one site/library/folder, identify the information inside by category, and name the HR or engagement owner who can decide who needs it.
2. **Compare actual and intended access.** Record how access is obtained: membership, inherited parent permission, direct grant, guest account or sharing link. Ask who still needs access for current work. Record legitimate partner, reviewer or deputy exceptions and expiry.
3. **Prioritise by consequence.** P1 means credible exposure of sensitive HR/client material to an unintended audience or a suspected incident. Escalate immediately through the firm's private route. P2 covers excessive inherited or guest access where immediate exposure is unconfirmed. P3 covers low-risk tidying and review dates. Use the firm's incident criteria where they are stricter.
4. **Hand over a specific request.** Include the observed condition and date, intended audience, precise proposed change, business owner approval, implementation owner, dependencies, potential loss of legitimate access, fallback and due date. Create one ticket per change that can be independently tested.
5. **Have IT verify both sides.** Using permitted test identities and harmless test material, an intended user can complete the required task and a role outside the intended audience cannot open the item via direct link, search or inherited access. Check actual sharing links and groups. The reviewer records IT's redacted evidence and owner acceptance.
6. **Retest Copilot separately.** Use a fresh authorised session and inspect any cited sources after the agreed propagation window. Restricting discovery can reduce what surfaces while an overly broad direct permission still grants access. A failed search is insufficient evidence of access removal. Keep the finding open if direct access remains or legitimate work breaks. [Microsoft Copilot data protection](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/architecture-data-protection-auditing) · [SharePoint restricted content discovery](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/restricted-content-discovery)

**Illustrative handoff.** HR-01 contains sensitive employee material and inherits a broad staff group. HR validates the intended HR group and one time-bound deputy. IT maps the inherited grant, selects the least disruptive change, verifies authorised access and checks that the unrelated role is denied through the direct link. If a payroll process loses access, IT follows the agreed recovery path and obtains HR validation. No real folder access was inspected or changed in the public example.
