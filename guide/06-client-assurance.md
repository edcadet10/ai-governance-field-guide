# Show clients and insurers what you actually checked

## Prepare a private evidence pack

1. Ask for the exact assurance question, business area, review period and delivery deadline. The engagement partner approves the audience and what can be disclosed.
2. Include the signed policy version, a summary of the use inventory and coverage gaps, the register of the five approved procedures, a redacted IT finding and retest, and one completed release review for a version that was actually sent. Keep personal identities, folder paths and client material private unless disclosure is authorised.
3. For each claim classify evidence as **designed** (a documented rule), **implemented** (an approved owner/configuration exists) or **observed** (a dated sample operated). Record the sample period, count, exceptions, source and responsible owner. A sample supports only the cases checked.
4. The designated reviewer chooses a sample from actual releases, including at least one material change or exception if available. Check the factual source, independent reviewer and version, approval time before dispatch, retention and any subsequent correction. Hold the statement if a necessary record is missing.
5. The partner and privacy lead review the redacted response, approve its specific version and retain the dispatch reference. Revisit open items at the next sponsor meeting. Use the firm's approved channel for sending it.

## Response to adapt after checking the evidence

“For [business area and dates], our approved policy [ID/version] assigns [role] to check AI-assisted client work before release. We examined [number] releases from [defined population]. [Number] had a dated review tied to the sent version. [Describe any exception and action.] The [process owner] maintains the current instructions in [approved location]. The IT lead verified [scope of access test] on [date]. [Outstanding limits and next review].”

If the firm only has a drafted policy, say exactly that and give the date of the first planned operating test. Never convert example records, an unsigned policy or an IT recommendation into a claim of completed operation.

## Monthly leadership questions

Which team or tool has unknown coverage? Which client releases lacked a reviewer? Which of the five process sources is overdue or contradicted? Did each urgent folder pass both the allowed and denied role tests? Has a client restriction changed? Record the decision to continue, revise, suspend or expand a use case, with a named owner and date. For UK adaptation, the privacy lead can use the ICO's accountability guidance as a starting point.
